Who We Are

From Home Baker
to Cafe Owner

The Skinny Baker began in a small home kitchen — late nights perfecting recipes, early mornings filling orders for friends and family. What started as a passion project quickly became something bigger, as word spread across Cebu City about our handcrafted cakes and sweet treats.

That growing demand turned a dream into reality. We took the leap from home-based baker to opening our very own cafe at One Tree Plaza Hotel Commercial Center in Banawa — a cozy space where customers can walk in, see our creations up close, and experience The Skinny Baker in person.

Every cake that leaves our counter still carries the same heart and attention from those early kitchen days. The only difference now? We have a place to share it with you.
