FAQ

We recommend placing orders at least 5 days before your desired pickup or delivery date. For complex designs or peak seasons, earlier is better.

We accept BPI bank transfer, GCash, and PayPal. A 50% down payment is required 5 days before the pickup/delivery date, with the remaining balance settled on the day of pickup/delivery.

Yes! We arrange delivery via Lalamove or Grab. Delivery fee will be sent the day before. We recommend Car delivery for cake safety. You can also book your own courier or pick up at our cafe.

Cancellations must be made at least 5 days before the delivery date. If a down payment was made, it will be converted to a Skinny Baker Cake Fund valid for 1 year. Late cancellations are not accepted.

After submitting your order form, send your design inspiration or reference photos to our Instagram or Facebook page. We'll confirm the design details with you directly.

Our base flavor is Chocolate Moist Cake (included in the price). Upgrades include Vanilla Buttercake (+PHP 100), Red Velvet (+PHP 150), Carrot Cake (+PHP 150), Moist Banana Cake (+PHP 150), and Matcha Buttercake (+PHP 200). Custom flavors are also available — just ask!
